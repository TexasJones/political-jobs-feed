Deputy Director, Support Transition (Term-Limited)

aclu

Location: New York, New York, United States

ABOUT THE JOB The ACLU seeks applicants for the full-time position of Deputy Director , Support Transition in the Development Department of the ACLU’s National office in New York, NY. This is a hybrid role that has in-office requirements of two (2) days per week or eight (8) days per month. This is an 18-month term-limited position. The ACLU’s passionate community of donors and activists is stronger than ever. The mission of the ACLU’s Development Department is to unlock the full potential of donors and the ACLU as partners in the creation of a more perfect union.
